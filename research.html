Research Interests

I develop machine learning methods, particularly graph neural network methods, for materials exploration. I have demonstrated ability to advance scientific discovery in data-scarce conditions through innovative architecture design, active learning, and cross-disciplinary collaboration. I am looking for a position in which I can leverage this expertise to drive breakthroughs in materials informatics and next-generation battery development.
